QZ Target Test

Point the TOP of the phone toward the unwanted noise. Touch and HOLD the arrow. While holding it, physically slide the entire phone toward the arrow. Do not drag the arrow on the screen. When QZ has enough directional information, the phone vibrates and displays GOT IT. After GOT IT, use START QZ AUDIO to begin the audio experiment. QZ Audio can then be switched ON and OFF without changing the target.
