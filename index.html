QZ TNC Direction Test

Place the phone flat with the top edge pointing toward the unwanted sound. Touch and hold the arrow, then push the phone toward the noise. QZ reads the phone motion sensors and determines when it has enough directional information. When QZ has the direction, the phone vibrates and displays GOT IT. This test proves the targeting gesture only. It does not yet perform targeted noise cancellation.
